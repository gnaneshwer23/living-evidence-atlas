# HSI — Scientific Intelligence for Human Biology

## MASTER WEBSITE DESIGN AND DEVELOPMENT BRIEF

Act as a senior product designer, creative director, scientific UX specialist, brand strategist and frontend engineer.

Design and build a premium, multi-page, responsive website for Human Super Intelligence (HSI).

Existing website: https://humansuperintelligence.tech/

This is not a generic AI startup, healthcare chatbot, literature search engine or professor-only research platform.

HSI is an emerging scientific intelligence ecosystem designed to connect human biology, scientific evidence, research data, persistent scientific memory and specialised AI agents.

The website must communicate the entire product vision while accurately distinguishing implemented capabilities, research prototypes and future developments.

### Brand positioning
Position HSI as: A scientific intelligence platform for human biology that unifies evidence, preserves institutional research memory, resolves scientific contradictions and accelerates discovery across multidisciplinary biomedical domains.

Tagline:
Understand biology. Resolve uncertainty. Advance discovery.

Alternative formulations:
- The intelligence layer for human biology.
- Scientific intelligence that remembers, connects and verifies.
- From fragmented literature to connected biological understanding.

Tone of voice:
- Rigorous, not sensationalist.
- Intellectually confident, not arrogant.
- Precise, not vague.
- Clear, not unnecessarily academic.
- Mission-driven, not opportunistic.

Never use generic marketing slogans such as:
- "Supercharge your research with AI."
- "The ChatGPT for scientists."
- "Revolutionise healthcare forever."

### Target users
1. Students & Early Career Researchers
2. Postdoctoral Researchers & Staff Scientists
3. Principal Investigators & Laboratory Directors
4. Academic Departments & University Research Centres
5. Biotechnology & Pharmaceutical R&D Teams
6. Translational & Clinical Researchers

### Visual identity and design system
The visual identity should evoke biological precision, digital intelligence and scientific permanence.

Color palette:
- Primary Dark: Deep Obsidian (#0B0F17) or Carbon Slate (#10141D)
- Primary Light: Crisp Chalk (#F7F9FB) or Clinical White (#FFFFFF)
- Accent 1 (Biological Intelligence): Cobalt Cyan (#0EA5E9) or Electric Azure (#2563EB)
- Accent 2 (Scientific Verification): Bio Green / Emerald Truth (#10B981)
- Accent 3 (Contradiction / Uncertainty): Amber Signal (#F59E0B)
- Accent 4 (Deep Genomics / Memory): Violet Helix (#8B5CF6)
- Surface Neutrals: Border Slate (#1E293B), Muted Fog (#64748B), Soft Card (#161F2E)

Typography:
- Display: Technical, refined sans-serif (e.g., Geist, Inter Display, Space Grotesk, Plus Jakarta Sans)
- Body: Neutral, highly legible sans-serif (e.g., Inter)
- Monospace / Evidence: JetBrains Mono or Roboto Mono for citations, identifiers, confidence metrics, gene symbols

### Website navigation
Desktop Header:
- Logo: HSI monogram with wordmark "Human Super Intelligence" and micro-label "Scientific Intelligence Platform"
- Navigation dropdowns:
  - Platform: Scientific Memory Engine, Scientific AI Agents, Knowledge Graph, Evidence Intelligence, Contradiction Analysis, Discovery Engine, Scientific Companion
  - Science: Immunology, Neuroscience, Microbiome & Metabolism, Genomics & Multi-omics, Organ & Tissue Biology, Disease Mechanisms
  - Solutions: Students & Early Researchers, Research Laboratories, PIs & Academic Leaders, Biotech & Pharma R&D, Translational & Clinical Science
  - Resources: How It Works, Scientific Data Sources, Benchmark & Evaluation, Trust & Governance, Research Programmes, Documentation
- Action buttons: "Request Early Access" (primary accent) and "Sign In" or "Explore Research Workbench"

Mobile Header: Clean responsive drawer preserving full architecture.

### Homepage structure
1. Immersive Hero: Headline "Understand biology. Resolve uncertainty. Advance discovery." Subhead on connected evidence, persistent memory, and specialized intelligence. CTAs: "Explore Platform", "Request Early Access", interactive preview toggle.
2. The Problem: Biology is connected. Research is fragmented. Exponential literature, trapped lab memory, unresolved contradictions, siloed tools.
3. The HSI Solution: Unified platform layer connecting 6 domains, persistent memory, and multi-agent reasoning.
4. Scientific Memory Engine: Showcase persistent lab memory, lineage tracking, contextual recall, cross-project synthesis.
5. Six Scientific Domains: Immunology, Neuroscience, Microbiome & Metabolism, Genomics & Multi-omics, Organ & Tissue Biology, Disease Mechanisms with interactive tabs.
6. Scientific AI Agents: Specialized biological agents collaborating (Literature Synthesis, Evidence Verification, Hypothesis Generation, Experimental Design, Contradiction Detection, Multi-omics Correlation).
7. Scientific Investigation Demonstration: Interactive walkthrough of a real biomedical inquiry (e.g., Gut-Brain Axis in Parkinson's or PD-L1 Resistance in Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer).
8. Contradiction Intelligence: Side-by-side evidence analysis showing how conflicting findings are evaluated and resolved.
9. Research Discovery Outputs: Evidence Dossiers, Mechanistic Hypotheses, Experimental Protocols, Knowledge Graphs.
10. Solutions for Every Research User: Tabbed personas (Students, PIs, Biotech R&D, Clinical).
11. Scientific Data Ecosystem: Integration with PubMed, bioRxiv, UniProt, PDB, ChEMBL, ClinVar, KEGG.
12. Scientific Companion: Ambient lab copilot / desktop companion.
13. Trust & Scientific Governance: Hallucination mitigation, source provenance, data sovereignty, human-in-the-loop validation.
14. Early Access & Research Collaboration: Clear conversion section and waitlist form.

### Required landing pages & architecture
Build out the core routes:
- `/` (Home)
- `/platform/memory` (Scientific Memory Engine)
- `/platform/agents` (Scientific AI Agents)
- `/platform/contradiction-analysis` (Contradiction Intelligence & Verification)
- `/platform/workbench` (Interactive Research Workbench preview)
- `/science` & domain exploration
- `/solutions` (Solutions overview with persona filtering)
- `/trust` (Trust, Governance & Evidence Provenance)
- `/early-access` (Early Access & Pilot Application)

Follow the 5 implementation stages and recommended order from the master prompt. Ensure accessible, responsive, sleek design inspired by Linear, Isomorphic Labs, Causaly, and Stripe.
